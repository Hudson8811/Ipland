specialisation

Since 2006, we have been providing legal assistance to Russian and global corporations, state-owned entities, private clients and not-for-profit organisations. Our key competences are to support complex investment projects and deals, and resolve complicated disputes, including cross-border and international ones.


We combine high proficiency in law with skills in providing legal protection to corporations before Russian public authorities and have unrivalled experience of managing large-scale, multi-jurisdictional projects.

selected experience

Advised a private Russian investment group on disposing of a 20% participatory interest in a Russian gas-producing company to a Chinese energy corporation, including structuring the deal and advising on regulatory issues. Deal value: $48 m

banking & finance

Advised Gazprombank, Sberbank and Otkritie bank on syndicated financing for a joint venture of the Sibur Holding, the biggest petrochemical company in Russia, and China Petroleum & Chemical Corporation (Sinopec), the world’s biggest petrochemical company, aiming construction of the Amur Gas and Chemical Complex in Russia’s Far East. Bridge-financing: $1.5 bn

Advised a major Russian bank on all legal aspects of loan financing to a Hong-Kong exchange listed producer of iron ore concentrate in the context of a project for developing one of Russia’s biggest ore deposits. Deal value: $240 m

Advising an international investment group as a borrower concerning several stages in the financing of a project in Uzbekistan for construction, overhaul and development of wells on oil and gas condensate fields. Total project investment: 1st stage financing: $200 m, 2nd stage financing: €180 m

Advised the developer and operator of a port in Russia’s Far East on attracting syndicated loan financing from VEB.RF, a major Russian bank. Credit line: ₽34 bn ($340 m)

regulatory

Advised a Fortune-500, Chinese state-owned energy corporation on Russian regulatory aspects of planned acquisition of an Israeli engineering company. Deal value: $120 m+

Advised a Chinese investor on all legal aspects of acquiring 100% of a Russian bank, including obtaining consent from the Bank of Russia and the Federal Antimonopoly Service

Advised Otkritie Holding, a Top-5 Russian finance group, on the regulatory aspects of acquiring from the Russian oil & gas major Lukoil 100% in a company operating Russia’s 2nd biggest diamond mine. Deal value: $1.45 bn

Advised Rockwell Capital investment fund on regulatory aspects of setting up, together with state development corporation VEB.RF, a joint timber-processing holding company, involving construction of an advanced wood-processing complex in Eastern Siberia. Matter value: ₽150 bn+ ($1,5 bn+)

infrastructure / energy / construction

Advised an international HKSE-listed gambling operator on investment in a construction and development project for a hotel-casino complex in the Primorye Gambling Zone in Russia’s Far East. Potential investment: up to $350 m

Advised a Russian company on a large-scale construction investment project for a new coal terminal in the Far East, including drafting and negotiating a contract with a Chinese engineering supplier for supply and installation of key equipment. Investment: ₽37 bn+ ($370 m)

Advising a private investor on a long-term project for increasing the capacity of the Gazli natural underground gas storage facility, the biggest in Uzbekistan. The project will help in increasing gas exports from Uzbekistan to China. Investment: up to $1 bn

Advised a Russian heavy industry major as an equipment supplier for a large-scale construction project for a copper concentrating complex in Uzbekistan. Contract value: up to $5 bn

international arbitration and domestic dispute resolution

Representing an international investment fund in arbitration proceedings over protection of the client’s investment in an oilfield service group in Russia, as well as enforcement procedures. Recovery amount: approximately $50 m

Represented UK investors, former shareholders in a Latvian bank, in a claim against the Republic of Latvia under a UK-Latvia BIT. Claimed amount: €70 m

Represented a road construction company in a precedent-setting dispute with the Federal Antimonopoly Service of Russia (FAS) concerning use of a concession agreement model for construction of a toll road. Project value: ₽20 bn+ ($200 m)
